# Research: Midwestern Positioning, Verticals & Competitive Landscape

**Compiled:** July 15, 2026, via research agent (WebFetch was blocked session-wide, so findings come from search-index synthesis of the live site and third-party sources — verbatim site copy should be spot-checked). Confidence levels flagged.

---

## 1. Ownership context

- **Jan 21, 2025:** Gloo Holdings (Nasdaq: GLOO) acquired an **80% stake** in Midwestern Interactive for ~$31.7M; Matthew Johnson remained CEO ([Religion News Service](https://religionnews.com/2025/01/21/gloo-announces-investment-in-software-product-powerhouse-midwestern/), [Gloo press release](https://gloo.com/press/releases/gloo-announces-investment-in-software-product-powerhouse-midwestern)).
- **June 8, 2026:** Gloo announced a definitive agreement to acquire the **remaining 20%** (100% wholly-owned; expected close Q2 2026); Midwestern continues under its own brand/leadership ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260608921666/en/Gloo-to-Acquire-Remaining-Stake-in-Tech-Talent-Powerhouse-Midwestern), [BizWest](https://bizwest.com/2026/06/11/gloo-takes-full-stake-in-midwestern-interactive/)).
- Press framing: Midwestern as a "prominent talent partner for the faith and flourishing ecosystem," bringing "Fortune 500 caliber technical expertise" into Gloo's ~450,000-org network; "home to more than 100 developers and product innovators."

## 2. Positioning & services (from indexed site copy — confidence: medium)

- Homepage title: **"Midwestern: Talent partner for the AI era of work."** Other indexed titles: "Flexible Talent Partner For Tech," "Recruiting Services: Hire Mission Aligned Talent Fast," "Embedded Teams: Flexible Talent Driven To Serve You."
- Self-description: "not just a product agency… a human capital engine"; mission "serve each other, serve our clients"; "13+ years… 120+ clients from Fortune 500 to early-stage startups… 250K+ hours annually on products that have raised over $450M."
- GibsonAI case-study framing: "senior talent who treat AI as an operating system, available scoped, embedded, or recruited, all globally sourced and US-led under one vendor" — note the global-sourcing/US-led framing.
- **Three service lines:** Embedded Teams, Scoped Projects (discovery, MVPs, technical validation), Recruiting (+ contract-to-hire).
- Read: a pivot from classic "custom software/digital product agency" (2012–2023) toward **tech talent/staffing partner** (2024–2026).

## 7. Competitive set & what visibly works for them

| Competitor | Model | Visible demand gen program |
|---|---|---|
| **Atomic Object** (Grand Rapids+) | Employee-owned custom software | **"Atomic Spin"** blog — employee-authored, ~daily cadence; 20+ year content moat |
| **Detroit Labs** | Custom software + staff aug (closest model match) | **Branded podcast ("Labs Live")** streamed live on LinkedIn/YouTube — proof a podcast works in this exact niche |
| **Sparkbox** (Ohio) | Web design/dev | **"The Foundry"** content hub (~750 articles) + newsletters + UnConference |
| **Hashrocket** | Dev consultancy | Apprenticeship-program storytelling |
| **Toptal** | Talent marketplace (scale benchmark) | Massive expert-bylined content hub |
| **X-Team** | On-demand embedded engineering | Global developer community (Slack), culture-led differentiation |
| **Gun.io / Turing** | Vetted marketplaces | Marketplace-trust mechanics; compete with the Recruiting line |
| **MentorMate** | Custom software + design | Standard content marketing |

**Cross-cutting takeaway:** the two strongest patterns among comparably-sized firms are (a) **named, high-frequency owned-content hubs** (Atomic Spin, The Foundry) and (b) **a genuine podcast tied to talent/culture storytelling** (Detroit Labs). Given Midwestern's "talent partner" pivot, the differentiated podcast lane is talent/culture/AI-era-of-work stories rather than another generic tech-leadership show.

A native dispatch app for 140 drivers, built and launched for a fixed price. Loads flow from your TMS to the cab without re-keying, and your team owns the code from day one.

The problem

The current driver app was built in 2017 and cannot read load changes from the TMS. Dispatchers re-key every change by hand, roughly 38 hours a week across four people.

Drivers work around it with texts and phone calls, so status in the TMS lags the road by hours.

Source: August ops review.

Our approach

We start with two weeks in your dispatch office and in the cab. Design is tested with drivers before build begins. Build runs in two-week releases to a pilot group of twelve drivers, then to the full fleet.

What you get

  • iOS and Android driver app with offline sync
  • Two-way integration with your TMS load API
  • Dispatcher web view for live status
  • Source code, runbook and release pipeline in your accounts

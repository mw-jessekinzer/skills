The challenge

Larkspur's four-person platform team owned the TMS, the customer portal and the driver app. The board wanted a new driver app by spring, and two open senior roles had sat unfilled for five months.

Client
Larkspur Freight, regional carrier
Service
Embedded team
Team

What we did

The team joined Larkspur's standup, worked from the same board and reviewed code with the in-house engineers. They shipped the offline-first driver app in two-week releases, first to a pilot group, then to the fleet.

# Output spec (S8)

`assemble.py` writes the CSV, the import file, and `work/08-summary.json`. A Sonnet worker writes `00 - Summary.md` from that JSON (numbers come from the JSON, never recounted by hand). Packets come from S6.

## Final CSV: `<YYYY-MM-DD> <Source label> Leads.csv`

UTF-8 with BOM, one row per canonical row, sorted by rank, plain-English headers:

Rank, Action, Verdict, Account strength, Why this verdict, Next step, Watch-outs, Name, Title, Company, Website, What the company does, Employees, Size band, Headcount source, US company, Hiring, Open tech roles (US), Example open roles, Need signals, Best service fit, Source, What they did, Touch date, Intent, CRM status, HubSpot owner, Suggested owner, Buyer to target, Email, Email status, LinkedIn, Confidence, City, State, Sources, Reason code, Lead ID.

- `Name` is "(company only)" on company rows; `Company` is never blank ("(unidentified)").
- `What they did` is the template phrase from `classify.py`, word for word.
- `Watch-outs` carries script notes a rep should see (today: tech roles that are all outside the US, which point to embedded global talent).
- `Suggested owner` is the active HubSpot owner, else "Jesse Kinzer" with the segment owner in brackets (fintech Kent Richeson; banks, insurance, wealth Derris Butler; Faith and Flourishing Spencer Hahn; proptech and construction Blake Fisher; cloud infrastructure Shiley Johnson; other Derris Butler). Blank on Drop rows.
- `Email status` uses only the ten statuses in `label-spec.md`; the word "verified" appears only for qualifying statuses.
- Text cells are 160 characters or fewer (Sources and Example open roles 300).

## HubSpot import CSV: `<YYYY-MM-DD> hubspot-import.csv`

Columns: `First Name, Last Name, Email, Job Title, Company Name, Associate to company domain, Contact owner, Priority, LinkedIn URL, Lead Generated By, Lead Status`.

JK's rule (2026-09-28):
- **New contacts only.** Rows are Contact now people plus Tier 1 committee members whose email status qualifies (`Verified`, `Verified, catch-all domain`, `Given by the person`). Anyone already a HubSpot contact is left out and listed under CRM follow-ups (importing them would overwrite `Lead Generated By` on records sales sourced). Anyone not checked against HubSpot is left out too.
- **Contact owner** = the company's active HubSpot owner; if the company is unowned or its owner is inactive, **Jesse Kinzer**.
- `Priority`: First call, Second wave, Third wave, Influencer; " (catch-all, deliverable per BounceBan)" is appended for that status.
- `Lead Generated By` = Marketing; `Lead Status` = New Marketing Lead; `Associate to company domain` = the verified domain.

This skill never imports anything. JK imports the file himself.

## Summary note: `00 - Summary.md`

Frontmatter:

```yaml
---
tags:
  - marketing
  - lead-triage
  - lead-qualification
  - <source tag, e.g. rb2b or linkedin-engagement>
date: YYYY-MM-DD
type: report
status: complete
project: "[[1_Projects/Lead Triage/00 - readme|Lead Triage]]"
summary: <one sentence>
---
```

Sections, in this order (headings start at `##`):

1. **Bottom line**: at most 5 bullets. Contact now names, Tell account owner flags, account plays and packets built, and the hit rate as "X of N rows".
2. **How to read the CSV**: Verdict = strict ICP result (Qualified, Possible match, Insufficient data, Not qualified). Action = what to do (Contact now, Tell account owner, Account play, Review, Low priority, Drop). Account strength = the company regardless of the person.
3. **Counts**: Verdict by Action table from `verdict_by_action`; the reconciliation line (source rows in window, merged, final rows).
4. **Contact now** and **Tell account owner**: one line each with the next step.
5. **Account plays**: a table of every account play; Tier 1 rows link their packet (`[[<Company> - Lead Packet]]`).
6. **Review queue**: one line each with the open item.
7. **CRM follow-ups**: `- [ ]` checkboxes from `crm_followups` (inactive owners, unowned records, name-only matches, contacts left out of the import).
8. **Data caveats**: the actual date range and zero-row days, what the source proves (RB2B: one page view per row, no UTMs, no submissions; social: reactions carry no timestamp; lists: how people joined), anything not checked.
9. **Vendor spend**: the `ledger.py summary` table.
10. **Rulings used**: the defaults JK can flip (personal email rule, TA titles are buyers, company-only rows cap at Insufficient data, ad landing pages from paid referrers read as Low intent, 18-month need window, offshore-only tech hiring counts as demand, missing evidence parks a row instead of dropping it, Tier 1 cap of 8).
11. **How this was built**: one paragraph.

Tier 1 companies in `enrichment_skipped` (from `work/06-enrich-skipped.csv`) are listed in the Account plays table with the reason and no packet link. Zero-row days are only reported when `run.json` profiled them; for social runs say the touch dates are scraper batch dates.

Run the humanizer skill on the note and packets, then `checks.py lint` on them.

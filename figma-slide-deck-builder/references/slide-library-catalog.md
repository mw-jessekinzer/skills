## Multi-column slides

| Layout | Key | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| 3 Column/Offer | `4092af82bc5a5d3bcf0debbb115fdd0c4619ec6a` | Three equal cards with numbered index, card title, description, bullet list, and icon. Best for presenting parallel service offerings, capability pillars, or options where each item is distinct but equal in weight — service tiers, engagement models, or product features that need side-by-side comparison without hierarchy. |
| 3 Column/Stepped | `bd9b2609a90991c99812254bade110f8d1030533` | Three equal cards with numbered index, card title, and single description — no bullets or icons. Includes a framing statement above the cards. Best for naming parallel problems, barriers, or insights where the framing statement sets context and each card unpacks one dimension of it. Lighter than the feature card variant — use when the point is the label, not a list of details. |
| 3 Column/Arch | `8c572f6d5490666e39c13cb13e11cf5be698473d` | Three open columns with title and description, connected by dashed arc lines and dot endpoints along the bottom — no card borders. Best for presenting sequential stages, a connected journey, or a service continuum where the relationship between items matters as much as the items themselves. Use over the card variant when you want to imply flow or progression rather than parallel and independent options. |

## Mapping source content to layouts

When converting external content to an MW deck, use this decision tree:

**Opening the deck:**
- Title with visual brand moment → Title Page/Wide Pane
- Title where the words carry the opening → Title Page/Big Illustration
- Bold opening claim or credibility statement → Entry Page
- Quote from leadership or mission alignment → Quote

**Structuring the deck:**
- Table of contents or agenda (3+ sections) → Table of Contents
- Section divider between major parts → Section Title

**Presenting concepts or offerings:**
- 3 parallel services, features, or pillars (with detail) → 3 Column/Offer
- 3 parallel problems, barriers, or insights (lighter) → 3 Column/Stepped
- 3 stages or connected journey (flow matters) → 3 Column/Arch
- 2 proof points with big numbers → 2 Column
- Paired definitions, beliefs, or principles → Clean + Simple
- High-density executive summary → Content + Bullets

**Showing data and process:**
- Growth trend with supporting context → Graph
- Phases or maturity levels (ascending) → Bar Chart
- Declining or changing value over time → Bar Chart (alt)
- Project timeline with phases and milestones → Gantt
- Strategic roadmap with compounding stages → Leaps
- Recurring KPIs with a target threshold → Target
- Goals or OKRs grouped by category → Rows

**Listing and enumerating:**
- Sequential process or phased approach → List
- Exhaustive feature/scope list (internal) → Initiatives
- Same list but client-facing with visual weight → Initiatives (alt)
- Cyclical or dual-track methodology → Product Process

**Showcasing people:**
- Team with hierarchy or featured leads → Team/6
- Evenly weighted team (5 people) → Staff/5
- Evenly weighted team (4 people) → Staff/4

**Using imagery:**
- Persona or customer pain with quote → Photo/Left
- Social proof with client logos (categorized) → Logos/Half Page
- Social proof with logos (volume speaks) → Logos/Full Page
- Visual break or work showcase (4 images) → Bento Box/4
- Image showcase with section label → Bento Box/6 Rightside
- Full visual impact moment → Bento Box/Full Masonry

**Closing the deck:**
- Final CTA with contact info → End Slide
